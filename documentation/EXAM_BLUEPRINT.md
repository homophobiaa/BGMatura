# Exam blueprint for the May 2027 session

This is the editorial record for issue #96. It collects what the official sources
establish about the compulsory state matura in Bulgarian language and literature
(ДЗИ по БЕЛ) for the **2027** target year, what they do not establish, and what the exam
configuration (#97) and exam content may therefore claim.

## What is established

### Dates for 2027 (order РД09-3343)

| Session | Compulsory ДЗИ по БЕЛ | Start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May – June | **19 May 2027** | 08:30 |
| August – September | **20 August 2027** | 08:30 |

The second compulsory exam is on 21 May and 23 August 2027. The additional exams are
26 May – 2 June and 24 – 27 August 2027. The same dates serve persons who finished the
last gymnasium grade before 2021/2022, and validation of competences.

### Exam type, duration, scoring (programme annex)

- The exam is written and anonymous.
- Duration is **four astronomical hours**.
- The maximum is **100 points**, recorded as a percentage of the maximum. The exam counts
  as passed at **30%** or more.
- Task kinds: multiple choice, short free response, extended free response, and text
  creation. Literature tasks use artistic texts that are not in the school programmes and
  that interpret one of the listed themes.
- The distribution of task kinds, cognitive levels, and points is **defined by the
  specification**, not by the annex.

### Content and competences (programme annex)

Four compulsory modules of the second gymnasium stage:

1. Language and society (Езикът и обществото)
2. Language uses (Езикови употреби)
3. Dialogic readings (Диалогични прочити), with nine themes: the native and the foreign;
   the past and memory; society and power; life and death; nature; love; faith and hope;
   work and creativity; choice and division
4. Critical reading (Критическо четене)

The annex lists the assessed competences per module. Quote them from the annex when
writing content, rather than from this summary.

### Structure of the latest exam: 20 May 2026

| Part | Time | Tasks | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60 minutes | 1–21 | 37 |
| 2 | 60 minutes | 22–40 | 33 |
| 3 | 120 minutes | 41 | 30 |
| **Total** | **240 minutes** | **41 tasks** | **100** |

- **Closed tasks** with four options and one correct answer: 22 tasks (1–7, 14–17, 24–34),
  one point each.
- **Other tasks**: 18 tasks (8–13, 18–23, 35–40) with short written answers, spelling and
  punctuation rewriting, filling gaps, matching, and short texts. Points range from 1 to 6.
  Task 21 and task 40 are short texts of up to five sentences, worth 6 points each.
- **Task 41** is one argumentative text of up to four pages on **one of two topics**: an
  essay or an interpretive composition on a given work. It is worth 30 points.
- Part 1 is language work: spelling, grammar, punctuation, and reading a text with a
  diagram. Part 2 opens with two vocabulary tasks (22–23) and then turns to literature
  (24–40). Part 3 is the essay or interpretive composition.
- The key gives only example answers for some open tasks (35, 36, 38) and states that
  adequate answers are accepted without word-for-word reproduction.

The key was read from the extracted PDF text, whose points column is shifted by one row for
the first tasks. The per-part totals were rebuilt task by task and add up to exactly 100,
which confirms them; recheck the table against the PDF before relying on one task's points.

## Conflicts between sources

- The programme annex says the exam has **31 tasks**. The 2026 exam has **41 tasks**.
  Either the annex was amended or it no longer describes the exam. The task count is
  therefore **not a fixed fact** for 2027.
- Some websites describe 40 test tasks worth 70 points plus one 30-point text. That matches
  neither the annex nor the 2026 per-part totals, and is not used.
- Search summaries of the order number and dates were checked against the order itself.

## Unresolved gaps

1. **No 2027 programme or specification found.** The programme annex found predates the
   2026 exam. Check МОН for the programme and specification for 2027 and compare them with
   the 2026 structure. The МОН page that lists exam materials for БЕЛ refused automated
   access (HTTP 403), so what it currently lists was **not checked**.
2. **Publication timing is unconfirmed.** A search summary claimed programmes must be
   approved by 31 January of the exam year. That was not found in Наредба № 11 and is not
   relied on.
3. **Scoring criteria for text tasks are missing.** The key gives only points for tasks 21
   (6), 40 (6), and 41 (30). The criteria and their weights were not found in the documents
   read.
4. **Cognitive-level distribution** and **duration for students with special educational
   needs** are required in the specification (Наредба № 11, Art. 58, para. 2) but were not
   found.
5. **Passing threshold for 2027.** The 30% comes from the annex; confirm it in the 2027
   documents.
6. **Curriculum reading lists.** The annex says literature tasks use texts outside the
   school programmes; the lists of required works must be taken from the current
   curriculum, which was not located here.

## Requirements for the exam configuration (#97)

- Store a configuration per **target year and session** and keep every version. A new
  specification adds a version; it never edits a historical one.
- Per configuration, store: target year and session, version, parts with their duration,
  tasks per part, task kinds, points per task or group, total maximum points, pass
  threshold, and a status of `verified`, `conditional`, or `unverified`.
- Store verification metadata with each version: primary source links, verification date,
  reviewer, and open gaps.
- Do **not** hard-code 41 tasks, 100 points, or 240 minutes in the interface or in code.
  Read them from the configuration, and test that several versions with different
  totals work.
- Seed a first version modeled on the 2026 exam and mark it **`conditional`** until the 2027
  specification is verified.
- Keep scoring rules explicit per task kind. Written texts follow the honest-assessment
  rule in [question grading](FEATURES/grading-service.md): no machine-assigned score.

## What the product may say

- **Allowed:** "Modeled on the structure of the 20 May 2026 official exam", with the source
  linked and the verification date shown.
- **Allowed:** the confirmed 2027 dates, linking the order.
- **Not allowed until the gaps are closed:** that practice exams match the 2027 exam, that
  a task count or point split is the 2027 one, or a predicted passing result.
- Content that quotes or adapts official exam material needs its attribution recorded (see
  question provenance in [DATABASE.md](DATABASE.md)).

## How to update this record

When the 2027 programme or specification appears, read it directly from МОН, update the
tables and the status block, resolve or restate each gap, change the configuration
status accordingly, and record the new verification date and reviewer. Do not copy
structure from an earlier year without checking it against the current documents.
